# User-supplied FNAF 1 sound bank

These WAVs were selected by the user from their local FNAF 1 sound extraction (source page: [Spriters Resource FNAF 1 sounds](https://sounds.spriters-resource.com/pc_computer/fivenightsatfreddys/asset/398089/)). The Downloads originals are retained. This project copies the selected files into its published `sound-bank/` directory.

The bank includes the menu theme; monitor up/down; camera; door and light controls; movement and spotted-at-light cues; regular and Golden Freddy jumpscares; the 6 AM and win cues; office ambience; and the low fan loop.
